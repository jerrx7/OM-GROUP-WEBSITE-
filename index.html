About the Group

OM Group of Companies is a Goa-rooted conglomerate of ten associate firms operating across construction aggregates trading, ready-mix concrete production, stone crushing, mineral logistics and heavy-equipment leasing.

Built on a foundation of trust over decades of disciplined execution, our operations span two modern RMC batching plants, a VSI stone crusher, and a managed fleet that keeps Goa's most ambitious projects on schedule.

We supply government, infrastructure and private clients with materials produced and delivered to specification — pairing the agility of a local operator with the standards of a national name.
