What Is Wet Mix Macadam (WMM)?

Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) is a pavement base-course material made by mechanically mixing clean, crushed, graded aggregate with a controlled amount of water in a wet mix plant, then spreading and compacting it into a dense pavement layer, in accordance with MoRTH Clause 406 and IRC:109.

It forms the base course above the granular sub-base (GSB) in a flexible pavement, and is produced from crushed fractions — nominally 40mm, 20mm, 10mm, 6mm and crusher-run screenings — proportioned by trial to a specified 0–53mm grading envelope, not supplied as a single aggregate size.

OM Group supplies the crushed, graded aggregate used in WMM production, produced at its own crushing operation and matched against MoRTH Clause 406 grading and the applicable project specification. OM Group operates the crusher that produces the coarse and fine fractions WMM is blended from — the wet-mixing operation itself (the pugmill/pan-mixer stage) is described on this page as the general WMM production process, not claimed as OM Group's own plant operation. What Goes Into WMM

A blend, not one aggregate size.

WMM is composed of crushed, graded aggregate fractions — nominally 40mm, 20mm, 10mm and 6mm, together with crusher-run screenings — proportioned by trial and error to achieve the gradation the applicable MoRTH table specifies. No single fraction is WMM on its own; the material only becomes WMM once the fractions are blended to the specified curve and mixed with controlled moisture.

OM Group's crushing operation at Banda Wafoli produces exactly these fractions as part of its multi-grade aggregate output — see the 20mm, 10mm and 6mm aggregate pages for each fraction's own specification. Proportioning these fractions into a WMM blend, and mixing that blend with controlled moisture, is a separate operation from producing the fractions themselves — see How OM Group Supports WMM Projects below for exactly where OM Group's role sits in that chain.

"40mm aggregate" is not WMM. A single crushed fraction, however clean or well-produced, is one input to a WMM blend — not the finished material.

How WMM Compares

Three materials it's often confused with.

WMM vs WBM

Different Method

Water Bound Macadam (WBM) is the older method of binding coarse aggregate with screenings and water, compacted on site. WMM is plant-mixed with controlled moisture and forced mixing, and carries stricter aggregate requirements — its Aggregate Impact Value limit (≤30%) is tighter than WBM's. WBM remains a recognised construction method under applicable specifications; its use depends on the project's specification rather than WMM being an automatic replacement.

Is Crusher-Run the Same as WMM?

Not the Same Material

No. Crusher-run and WMM are not automatically the same material. WMM requires controlled proportioning to the specified grading envelope and controlled moisture mixing at a wet mix plant; crusher-run describes a mixed-size crusher output and does not by itself establish compliance with the WMM specification. Uncontrolled multi-size crusher output only becomes WMM once it is proportioned to the Clause 406 curve and mixed with controlled moisture.

WMM vs Single-Size Aggregate

Cannot Replace WMM

40mm, 20mm, 10mm and 6mm aggregate are each one particle size — inputs to a WMM blend, not WMM itself. WMM is a blend proportioned to a single 0–53mm grading envelope, not any one fraction supplied alone. Ordering a single aggregate size and using it as WMM does not meet Clause 406's grading requirement.

WMM Grading & Particle-Size Distribution

One envelope, verified per contract.

WMM uses a single grading envelope, nominally 0–53mm, per the applicable MoRTH table. Individual fractions are proportioned by trial and error to achieve that gradation — a stockpile of any one fraction is not itself WMM, and grading compliance is checked on the blended, mixed material.

Table numbering for the WMM grading and physical requirements varies across sources and editions (400-8, 400-10, 400-12 and 406.2.1.2 all appear in current references). This page cites Clause 406 as the controlling reference; verify the applicable MoRTH revision and project/contract specification before treating any single table number as the controlling requirement — engineering standards are periodically revised.

Construction requirements referenced under Clause 406: a single compacted layer not less than 75mm, and up to 200mm with vibratory compaction; OMC determined per IS 2720 Part 8; lateral confinement required during laying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered directly.

Basic

What is WMM?

WMM (Wet Mix Macadam) is a pavement base-course material made by mechanically mixing clean, crushed, graded aggregate with controlled water in a wet mix plant, then compacting it into a dense layer, per MoRTH Clause 406.

What does WMM stand for?

WMM stands for Wet Mix Macadam.

What is Wet Mix Macadam?

Wet Mix Macadam is the full name for WMM — a crushed, graded aggregate blend, mixed with controlled water and compacted to form a pavement base course, specified under MoRTH Clause 406 and IRC:109.

What is WMM used for?

WMM is used as the base course in flexible pavements for highways, roads, industrial and infrastructure projects, placed above the granular sub-base (GSB) and below the bituminous or surface layer.

Is WMM a base course?

Yes. WMM is generally used as the base course, above the granular sub-base and below the bituminous or concrete surface, subject to the project's pavement design.

Technical

What grading does WMM require?

WMM uses a single 0–53mm grading envelope per the applicable MoRTH table. Table numbering varies across sources and revisions, so the specific table and values should be verified against the applicable contract/MoRTH revision.

What is MoRTH Clause 406?

MoRTH Clause 406, in Section 400 of the Specifications for Road and Bridge Works, is the principal Indian reference governing WMM production, grading and construction requirements.

What is IRC:109?

IRC:109-2015 is the Indian Roads Congress guideline for Wet Mix Macadam, covering design and construction guidance alongside MoRTH Clause 406.

What is the Aggregate Impact Value requirement for WMM?

A general standard requirement of ≤30% is cited under MoRTH Clause 406 — stricter than WBM. This is a specification reference, not an OM Group test result for a specific consignment.

What is the Los Angeles Abrasion requirement for WMM?

A general standard requirement of ≤40% is cited under MoRTH Clause 406. This is a specification reference, not a claim about a specific tested consignment.

What is the flakiness and elongation requirement for WMM?

A general standard requirement of ≤30% combined flakiness and elongation index is cited under MoRTH Clause 406.

What is the plasticity index requirement for WMM?

A general standard requirement of ≤6, for the fraction passing 425 micron, is cited under MoRTH Clause 406.

What is WMM layer thickness?

A single compacted WMM layer is generally not less than 75mm, and up to 200mm with vibratory compaction, per Clause 406 — confirm against the project's pavement design.

How is WMM compacted?

WMM is compacted after mixing and spreading, with lateral confinement during laying and moisture controlled near the Optimum Moisture Content (OMC) determined per IS 2720 Part 8.

Why is moisture control important in WMM?

Moisture content close to OMC is what allows the material to reach its target compacted density — placement too wet or too dry of OMC falls short of the density the pavement design assumes, regardless of grading quality.

Comparison

What is the difference between WMM and GSB?

GSB is the sub-base layer, governed by MoRTH Clause 401; WMM is the base course above it, governed by Clause 406, more rigorously graded and plant-mixed with controlled moisture. They are not interchangeable.

Is WMM the same as WBM?

No. WBM (Water Bound Macadam) is the older on-site binding method; WMM is plant-mixed with controlled moisture and forced mixing, and carries a stricter Aggregate Impact Value limit.

Is crusher-run the same as WMM?

No. Crusher-run is an uncontrolled mixed-size crusher output. WMM requires controlled proportioning to the specified grading envelope and controlled moisture mixing — crusher-run does not by itself establish compliance with the WMM specification.

Is WMM the same as 40mm aggregate?

No. 40mm aggregate is one crushed fraction — an input to a WMM blend, not WMM itself. WMM is a blend proportioned to a 0–53mm grading curve, not a single size.

Can GSB replace WMM?

No. GSB and WMM occupy different, specified positions in the pavement structure, governed by different MoRTH clauses. One cannot be substituted for the other without engineering approval.

Can crusher-run replace WMM?

Not as supplied. Crusher-run must first be proportioned to the Clause 406 grading and mixed with controlled moisture before it meets the WMM specification.
