Construction Materials Supplier in Goa

OM Group supplies crushed stone aggregates, manufactured and plaster sand, ready-mix concrete, granular sub-base and related road materials across North and South Goa. Aggregates are produced at the Banda Wafoli VSI facility and ready-mix concrete is batched at the Kundaim and Thivim plants.
