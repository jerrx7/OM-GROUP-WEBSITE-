Crushed stone aggregates come from the VSI crushing facility at Banda Wafoli. Ready-mix concrete is batched at the Kundaim and Thivim plants operated by Sabsol Qualitech Industries LLP. Not every group company produces every material, and each product page states how that material is supplied.
