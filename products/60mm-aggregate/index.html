Is 60mm an IS 383 nominal aggregate size?

No. IS 383:2016 lists 40mm and 63mm as nominal coarse-aggregate sizes. 60mm is a commercial aggregate description used within the broader 40–63mm size range, so the actual grading should be checked against sieve analysis and the project specification.

60mm is one of the largest crushed-stone grades OM Group supplies, specified where bulk, void size and economy matter more than fine placement — large mass pours, heavy drainage media and specialised foundation or fill work. OM Group produces 60mm aggregate through its own stone-crushing operation and supplies it to construction sites and project contractors across North and South Goa.

What Is 60mm Aggregate?

60mm aggregate is a large-size crushed stone grade used for heavy-duty foundation, drainage, mass-fill and specialised construction work.

IS 383:2016, the Indian standard for aggregates in concrete, lists 63mm as its largest defined coarse-aggregate nominal size. 60mm is a trade grade that sits within this large-aggregate range — it is supplied against project and trade specification rather than as a distinct IS 383 nominal size in its own right. Confirm the exact grading requirement with your engineer or project specification before ordering.

Larger aggregate presents less surface area per unit volume than smaller grades, which is generally why large-size aggregate is specified for bulk fill, drainage media and mass-concrete work where cement economy and large void size matter more than fine placement around reinforcement.

In Indian construction trade usage, terms such as metal or blue metal are commonly used for crushed stone aggregate generally; "60mm" in trade conversation refers to this large-size grade. Terminology and exact size cut-offs vary by region and supplier, so confirm the size specification directly rather than relying on trade names alone when ordering.

60mm Aggregate Size and Grading

A large-aggregate grade, confirmed against the project.

Because 60mm sits above IS 383:2016's largest listed nominal size (63mm being the closest standard reference), grading and physical requirements for a 60mm supply are generally set by the specific project specification or tender document rather than a single universal table. Where a project cites a specific standard or grading envelope for large/oversize aggregate, that document governs.

Coarse aggregate at this scale is generally assessed on particle shape, cleanliness and freedom from deleterious material, water absorption, specific gravity, and mechanical performance through crushing, impact and abrasion testing, using the relevant IS 2386 methods — the same general test framework applied to OM Group's other aggregate grades.

How 60mm Aggregate Compares

Three materials it's often confused with.

60mm Aggregate vs GSB

Not Interchangeable

60mm aggregate is a single large-size coarse-aggregate grade; GSB (Granular Sub-Base) is a blended, graded sub-base material meeting a sieve/grading curve under MoRTH Clause 401. A 60mm fraction is not GSB on its own and cannot substitute for it without being blended to the specified curve. See the GSB page for the full specification.

60mm Aggregate vs WMM

Not Interchangeable

Wet Mix Macadam (WMM) is a plant-mixed, moisture-conditioned base-course material under MoRTH Clause 406, produced by blending multiple smaller aggregate fractions with water to a specified grading. 60mm's large particle size sits outside WMM's typical input range. See the WMM page for what goes into the mix.

60mm Aggregate vs Boulders

Different Product

60mm aggregate is processed, crushed and screened to a large but sized fraction. Boulders are substantially larger individual stones, generally used whole rather than as a sized aggregate fraction, in applications such as pitching, gabion work and soling. They are not interchangeable products. See the Boulders page for boulder types and applications.

Not Sure Which Size You Need?

Follow your engineer's specification first.

If your engineer or project specification has specified the aggregate size, follow that specification. Aggregate size is a design decision and nothing on this page substitutes for it.

If the requirement is still being assessed, these are the questions worth putting to your engineer:

  • Is this large-scale mass fill, drainage media, or a heavy foundation? This generally points toward 60mm or another large-size grade.
  • Would 40mm meet the same requirement at a smaller, more common size? Compare against the 40mm aggregate page.
  • Is any part of this going into reinforced concrete? Confirm member dimensions and reinforcement spacing against IS 456:2000 before specifying a large grade.
  • Does your tender or contract document specify an exact large-aggregate grading? That document governs over any general reference.

Share the application and project details with your enquiry and OM Group can discuss the material requirement and quantities with you.
