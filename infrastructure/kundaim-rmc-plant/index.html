What the Kundaim RMC Plant Is

Part of OM Group's manufacturing infrastructure in Goa.

The Kundaim RMC Plant is part of OM Group's construction-materials manufacturing infrastructure in Goa, producing and dispatching ready-mix concrete under controlled batching for projects that need consistent material proportioning and coordinated site delivery. RMC offers advantages over site-mixed concrete where mix proportions need tight control, timelines are demanding, large volumes need coordinated delivery, or multiple pours require repeatable material quality — it is not claimed to be the right choice for every situation.

Plant Capacity

120 m³/hour — rated batching capacity.

The Kundaim facility is equipped with Schwing Stetter batching infrastructure rated at up to 120 cubic metres per hour, providing substantial production capability for project-scale concrete requirements.

Batching Equipment

Schwing Stetter batching infrastructure.

Schwing Stetter batching infrastructure at Kundaim supports controlled, repeatable proportioning and high-volume project capability. Equipment alone does not determine finished-product quality — that comes from equipment, materials, mix design, calibration, process control, testing and trained personnel together, not the batching plant brand on its own.

Manufacturing Entity & OM Group Relationship

OM Group is the brand. Sabsol Qualitech is the manufacturer.

RMC at Kundaim is manufactured through Sabsol Qualitech Industries LLP, operating as part of OM Group's broader construction-materials infrastructure. Sabsol Qualitech Industries LLP also operates OM Group's RMC batching plant at Thivim and a VSI stone-crushing facility at Banda Wafoli — batched with UltraTech Cement, consistent with OM Group's wider RMC operations across Goa.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered directly.

Where is the Kundaim RMC Plant located?

The Kundaim RMC Plant is located in Kundaim, North Goa. Use the Get Directions link on this page for the exact location.

What is the rated batching capacity of the Kundaim plant?

The Kundaim plant is equipped with Schwing Stetter batching infrastructure rated at 120 m³/hour. This is a rated batching capacity, not a guarantee of hourly output or daily production.

What batching equipment is used at Kundaim?

Schwing Stetter batching infrastructure is used at the Kundaim RMC plant.

Who operates the Kundaim facility?

The Kundaim RMC plant is operated through Sabsol Qualitech Industries LLP, part of OM Group's broader construction-materials infrastructure.

Which RMC grades are available at Kundaim?

Grade availability at Kundaim is confirmed against current production and your project requirement. OM Group's full grade range is covered on the Ready-Mix Concrete product page.

Can I request a project-specific mix design?

Yes. Share your engineer-approved mix design and grade requirement with your enquiry and OM Group will confirm against current production.

What types of projects can the Kundaim facility support?

Residential, commercial, industrial and infrastructure projects — as an application category, not a claim that every project type has been completed to date.

Does OM Group supply RMC across Goa from Kundaim?

Yes, subject to project location, scheduling and logistics. Supply feasibility is assessed per project rather than guaranteed by a fixed delivery radius.

Is supply to North Goa possible from Kundaim?

Yes. Kundaim is a North Goa facility, positioned to support dispatch coordination across the region, subject to project logistics.

Is supply to South Goa possible from Kundaim?

Supply feasibility to South Goa is assessed per project, based on location, quantity, grade, schedule and logistics.

How is RMC delivered from Kundaim?

RMC is delivered by transit mixer from the Kundaim plant to the project site.

How do I request an RMC quotation for a Kundaim-supplied project?

Use the Request RMC Quote form on this page, call OM Group directly, or submit a project-specific enquiry through the quotation section.

What information is needed for a quote?

Grade or requirement, estimated quantity, project location, required delivery date, and any pumping or scheduling requirements. Company and contact details help OM Group respond faster.

Can engineers request technical documentation?

Yes. Technical documentation can be requested on a project basis, subject to what is current for the material being supplied.

Does 120 m³/hour mean 120 m³ is delivered every hour?

No. 120 m³/hour is the plant's rated batching capacity, not a guaranteed hourly delivery volume. Actual dispatch depends on scheduling, mix design, transport and site conditions.

How is large-volume RMC supply scheduled from Kundaim?

Large-volume and recurring requirements are coordinated against production scheduling, fleet availability, site readiness and project requirements. Share your project's volume and schedule with your enquiry to discuss the supply plan.
