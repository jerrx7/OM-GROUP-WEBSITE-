What Sancoale Currently Is

A Heavy Machines base, not (yet) an RMC plant.

OM Group's Sancoale location is a Heavy Machines deployment base for South Goa projects, part of the fleet operations run by Sabsol Heavy Machines LLP — JCB excavators and backhoe loaders, transit mixers and tippers, deployed for project leasing and turnkey site operations across Kundaim, Thivim and Sancoale.

Ready-mix concrete manufacturing at Sancoale is not currently part of OM Group's confirmed offering. This page will be updated if and when that changes. For confirmed RMC supply today, OM Group's batching plants are at Kundaim and Thivim.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered directly.

Is Sancoale an RMC plant?

Not currently. Sancoale is a Heavy Machines deployment base for South Goa projects. OM Group's confirmed RMC batching plants are at Kundaim and Thivim.

What does OM Group do at Sancoale?

Sancoale is a deployment base for OM Group's Heavy Machines fleet — JCB excavators, backhoe loaders, transit mixers and tippers — supporting South Goa projects.

Does OM Group supply RMC to South Goa?

Yes, subject to project location, scheduling and logistics — dispatched from OM Group's Kundaim or Thivim RMC plants. Supply feasibility is assessed per project.

Can I hire heavy machinery through the Sancoale base?

Yes. Enquire with your equipment requirement and project location, and OM Group will confirm availability.
