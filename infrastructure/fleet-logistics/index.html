Fleet & Logistics Capability

What OM Group's fleet supports.

Material dispatch — aggregates, sand and other construction materials moving from source to project site. Heavy-machinery deployment — supporting quarrying, crushing and site operations. RMC delivery coordination — transit mixer scheduling linked to Kundaim and Thivim's batching operations. General vehicle categories referenced, not an exact count: tippers, trucks (including larger multi-axle vehicles for bulk loads), transit mixers, and heavy equipment such as excavators and loaders used in quarry and site operations.

A project's material supply depends on more than what's available at the source — transport capacity, scheduling and route coordination determine whether material actually arrives when a project needs it. OM Group's logistics coordination is part of what its material supply pages already describe; this page exists to make that capability visible on its own.
