Actual production varies with feed material, product specification, plant configuration, operating conditions, maintenance and production scheduling — the installed figure describes the plant's designed capability, not a guaranteed hourly output.

VSI Technology

Controlled impact, consistent process.

Vertical shaft impact crushing reduces material through controlled impact and can contribute to particle shape depending on feed and configuration. Final aggregate characteristics depend on the complete process — feed material, crusher configuration, operating parameters, screening and quality control together, not the crushing technology alone.
